Tend privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026. Tend is a game in which a pet lives or dies by whether you show up: a walk, a workout, a weigh-in. This page says plainly what the app reads, what it keeps, and what it never does.

What stays on your phone

  • Health data. Steps, workouts and weight are read from Apple Health or Health Connect with your permission and used on the phone to feed the pet. Raw steps and weights are never uploaded. What leaves the phone, only when you have an account, is a daily share: whether you showed up, a fraction of a day, minutes of training, and dollars you said you kept if you use the jar.
  • The camera. Reading your scale happens on the phone. No photo is saved and nothing is sent. A food barcode scan sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to look up the food, nothing else.
  • Location. Only while the walk screen is open, only if you allow it, to draw the path you walked. It is never stored and never sent. Decline it and the path is invented.
  • Face ID or fingerprint. Handled by your phone. Tend never sees it.

What we keep on the server, with an account

  • Your email, your username, your birth year (to set a fair daily target), the daily shares above, your pet and its history as a backup, your friends, requests, cheers, nudges, gifts, and chat messages with friends (kept 30 days).
  • Crash reports and simple usage events such as "opened the app" or "started a walk", so we can see where the app breaks and where people stop. No content, no health numbers.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Read your bank. Money in the jar is what you tell us you kept.
  • Upload photos of you, your scale, or your screen.
  • Show ads in the game beyond one optional rewarded video a day, which you choose to watch, and which never affects the pet.

Who can see your profile

You choose: public, friends only, or private. Everyone can see your pet, its name, your username, your rank, weeks active and banners. The rest is blurred or hidden as you set it. Chat is between friends only, filtered for abuse, and every message can be reported or the sender blocked.

Age

Tend is for people 18 and over. We ask your age at setup and do not knowingly keep data from anyone younger.

Your choices

Sign out and everything stays on your phone. Delete your account in Settings and the server copy is removed within 30 days. Revoke health, camera or location access any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working with less.
