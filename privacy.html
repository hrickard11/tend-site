Tend privacy policy

Effective and last updated September 26, 2026.

Tend is a game for adults in the United States. Your pet grows with your real health: your steps, your workouts, and, if you choose, your weight, your sleep and the food you log. This page says plainly what Tend collects, where it comes from, why, who it goes to, how long we keep it, and what you can do about all of it, wherever in the US you live.

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy covers your health data in particular, as Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other states require. It's part of this policy.

The short version

  • Tend asks for two things separately when you set it up: your OK to use your health data (needed to play), and your OK to share your activity with friends (optional, and off unless you turn it on).
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for advertising. We don't do targeted advertising and we don't build profiles about you.
  • Ads never get your health data. Free players see at most one short ad a day, never personalized, and never over a health screen. Tend+ has no ads.
  • Your health data is never stored in iCloud and never used for anything but running Tend for you.
  • You can download everything, turn off either consent, or delete your account anytime in the app: Settings, then Your privacy.
  • Tend collects no biometrics. Face ID, if you use it to lock the app, stays with Apple; Tend never sees it.

Why we use it

  • To run the game you asked for: to feed and grow your pet, set fair goals, show your progress, give treats and milestones, and keep your game safe by backing it up to your account.
  • For the friend features you choose: friends, chat, rooms, and, only with your OK to share your activity, shared goals, pairs, standings and exercising together.
  • To keep Tend working and safe: to fix crashes, see where the app confuses people, stop cheating and abuse, and answer reports.
  • To send notifications you turned on, like reminders and friends' cheers.
  • To follow the law, and to answer requests about your data.

We collect only what these need, and we use your health data only for them. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects (like credit, insurance, housing or jobs).

Your two choices

  • Let Tend use your health data. Asked on its own screen at setup, before Tend reads anything or sends anything. Tend can't work without it, since your health is what feeds your pet. Turn it off anytime in Settings, then Your privacy: Tend stops reading and backing up right away and your pet waits until you turn it back on. You can also delete your account there.
  • Share your activity with friends. A separate choice, off unless you turn it on. With it on, friends see how many weeks you've been active, your streak and milestones, the last day you exercised, and whether you met your goal each day; a friend you share a goal with sees your daily total; a friend you exercise with sees that session's steps and minutes while it's happening. Turn it off anytime, and we clear what friends can see.

You can also turn off Health, motion, location, camera or notification access anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

We don't sell or share your data

Tend does not sell your personal information or your health data, and does not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California and other states define those words. We don't do targeted advertising and we don't profile you. We have not done any of these in the past 12 months. Because there's nothing to opt out of, there's no "Do Not Sell or Share" switch to find, but you can always write to us, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals and any opt-out request anyway. We don't knowingly sell or share the data of anyone under 16 (or anyone at all).

Ads, for free players only

  • What there is. One optional video a day you choose to watch for extras, and at most one short ad a day, only as you close a session's summary: never during a session, never in your first week, never after a session under five minutes, and never over a health screen (Your health, the food log, weigh-ins). Tend+ has no ads.
  • What ads get. Every ad request asks Google AdMob for non-personalized ads with restricted data processing (Google's setting for US state privacy laws). It carries no keywords, no page, and nothing about you, your pet or your health. Google's ad software does collect device and connection information to show and count an ad; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • No tracking. Tend doesn't ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple Health

Tend reads from Apple Health only what you allow, and only to run the game. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with anyone except as this policy says (and never with advertisers or data brokers), and never stored in iCloud. The iPhone app only reads Apple Health. The Tend app on your Apple Watch saves the workouts you record with it to Apple Health (with their energy and distance), as any workout app does.

What other players see

  • Anyone who finds you: your username, your pet, its name and look, and your profile setting. If your profile is public and you share your activity, they can also see your weeks active, tier and milestones.
  • Your friends, always: the name you pick, your pet and your room (its layout, your pet's look and its bond level).
  • Your friends, only if you turn on sharing: weeks active, streak, tier (from your average steps), milestones and badges, the last day you exercised, whether you met your goal each day, what's on your room's shelf (a keepsake for each week you kept, gifts from places you reached), your daily total in a shared goal, your name and streak in a pair, and a live session's steps and minutes while you exercise together (not stored).
  • If your profile is private: only your friends see anything. You don't show up when people search. Someone who has your invite link sees just your username, your pet's name and what kind of animal it is, so they can ask to be friends.
  • A friend whose room you visit: that you visited, and when.
  • Chat is between friends only, filtered for abuse, and every message can be reported or its sender blocked.

How we protect it

Everything travels encrypted. On our server, rules let each account read only its own backup, album and messages, and friends only what this page says they can. You can lock Tend with Face ID. No system is perfect, which is why we also promise the next section.

If something goes wrong

If there's ever a breach of your health data or other personal information, we'll tell you without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after we find out (sooner where a state requires it), by email and in the app. We'll say what happened, what data was involved, what we're doing about it, and what you can do. We'll also tell the Federal Trade Commission and any state that requires it, as the FTC's Health Breach Notification Rule and state laws ask.

Your rights, wherever in the US you live

Many states (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia and Washington) give people rights over their data. We give them to everyone:

  • To know and to see what we have about you, how we got it, why, and who we shared it with (including a list of every third party). In the app: Settings, then Your privacy, then Download my data.
  • To take it with you, in a file you and other apps can read (the same download).
  • To correct it. Most things you can change in the app; write to us for anything else.
  • To delete it. In the app: Settings, then Delete my account. Or write to us.
  • To withdraw your consent to either choice, anytime, in Settings, then Your privacy.
  • To opt out of the sale of your data, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't do any of them, and you can still ask.
  • To limit the use of sensitive information, like health data. We already use it only to run Tend for you.
  • To be treated the same whether or not you use these rights.

How to ask

Email hrickard26@gmail.com, or use Contact us in the app. Write from your account's email, or tell us your username, so we can check it's you; we'll only ask for what we need to confirm that. Someone you authorize (an authorized agent) can ask for you with your signed permission. We'll answer within 45 days. If we need more time (up to 45 more days, where the law allows), we'll tell you why. Requests are free.

How to appeal

If we turn down your request, reply to our answer and ask us to look again. We'll answer within 45 days and explain why. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general. For example: Washington, atg.wa.gov/file-complaint; Colorado, coag.gov/file-complaint; Connecticut, portal.ct.gov/ag; Virginia, oag.state.va.us/consumer-protection. In California, you can also contact the California Privacy Protection Agency at cppa.ca.gov.

Do Not Track

Tend doesn't track you across other apps or websites, so a browser's Do Not Track signal doesn't change anything we do. This website has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Age

Tend is for adults 18 and over. We ask the year you were born before anything else is collected, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it. If you think a child has used Tend, write to us.

No biometrics

Tend doesn't collect, store or use biometric identifiers or information: no face geometry, fingerprints, voiceprints or retina scans. If you lock Tend with Face ID, your iPhone checks your face and only tells Tend yes or no.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, like using your data in a new way, we'll tell you in the app first, and for health data we'll ask for your OK again before it applies.
