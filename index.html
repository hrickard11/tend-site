How Tend works

Your workouts feed it

Your pet runs on what you actually do: your steps on cardio, your sessions on training. Reach your goal and it's fed for the day.

Your weeks grow it

Pick how many days a week you want to move. Make your week and your pet grows up, from a little one to a champion.

Rest is part of it

Off days are rest, not failure. Miss a while and your pet just naps until you're back. It never leaves.

Your pet and the whole game are free. Tend+ adds a second companion, the full plan library and deeper stats.
