Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated October 16, 2026.

This policy is for Tend, a game for adults in the United States made by Tend With Me LLC, a Kentucky limited liability company. It covers "consumer health data" as Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), Connecticut's consumer health data rules and similar laws define it: information that's linked or reasonably linkable to you and identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health. We apply it to everyone who uses Tend, wherever you live. Our privacy policy covers everything else.

The consumer health data we collect

  • Steps, exercise minutes and workouts.
  • Your sleep (time asleep, sleep stages, and when you fell asleep and woke) and your resting heart rate.
  • The sessions you do in Tend: time, distance over time, steps, mile splits and estimated calories. The route of an outdoor session stays on your phone for that workout's map and is never sent to us.
  • When you open a workout: its heart rate, flights climbed and running form, read on your phone to show that workout. Your heart rate during and after a session is shown to you, not kept.
  • Weight and height if you enter them, goal weight, and whether you want to lose, keep or gain weight.
  • The food you log and its calories, and your favorite and saved foods.
  • The sets and workouts you log (your strength logs).
  • Rest days, and days you mark yourself sick or hurt.
  • What the game makes of all this: your pet's growth and stage, your streaks, weeks active, milestones and tier, and things like "well rested".

We don't collect biometric data, or any data about reproductive or sexual health, gender-affirming care, or health conditions beyond what's listed here. The precise route of a session stays on your phone and is never sent to us. Tend never sets up geofences around health care locations.

Where it comes from

  • Apple Health, with your permission (including what other apps, smart scales and your Apple Watch write there).
  • Your iPhone's motion sensor and your Apple Watch, during a session.
  • Your iPhone's location, only if you allow it: used on your phone during a session you start (including in the background with your phone in your pocket, while iOS shows its location indicator) so your pet follows your path and the trail's weather matches yours. The route stays on your phone for the workout's map and is never sent to us; if Save workouts to Apple Health is on, the route is saved to Apple Health with the workout. For weather, a position rounded to about two-thirds of a mile (1 km) goes to Apple Weather (WeatherKit).
  • What you type into Tend.

Why we collect and use it

  • To run the game: to feed and grow your pet, set fair goals, show your progress, check each day's result, and give treats and milestones.
  • To back your game up to your account, so it isn't lost and can come with you to a new phone.
  • For the friend features you choose, and only with your separate OK to share your activity.

We never use it for advertising and never sell it. Usage counts and crash reports, which you can choose to send to help improve Tend, never hold your steps, weight, food, sleep or heart rate. Some of those events are about your activity: that you met your goal for the day, finished a workout and which kind, logged a weigh-in or a meal, or booked a rest day. They say that it happened, never the numbers. Tend never puts your health data in iCloud itself. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud, Apple's encrypted device backup includes Tend's files on your phone.

Your consent

Tend asks for three separate consents on their own screen, before it reads or sends anything:

  1. To collect and use your health data for the purposes above. It's needed to play, since your health is what feeds your pet. We updated the words of this consent in October 2026, so if you played before then, Tend asks you once more.
  2. To share your activity with friends. Optional, and off unless you turn it on. Asked separately, and never bundled with anything else.
  3. To help improve Tend by sending usage counts tied to your account (which screens open, whether a session finished, crashes), never your steps, weight, food, sleep or heart rate. We updated these words in October 2026, because they used to call the counts anonymous, so if you'd said yes before then, Tend asks you once more. Optional, and off unless you turn it on. Without it, nothing is counted, queued or sent, and crash reports stay on your phone. Anything an older version of Tend queued before this choice existed is deleted, never sent.

Each is recorded with its date and the version of the words you agreed to, and you can see them in Settings, then Your privacy. You can withdraw any of them there anytime. Withdrawing stops the collection or sharing right away; it doesn't delete what's already stored, which you can delete there too. Turning off Help improve Tend also throws away anything waiting to be sent.

We don't share consumer health data with advertisers or data brokers. Tend has no ads and no ad software. We have no affiliates. We may disclose it if the law requires it, and we'd tell you if the law allows.

We never sell it

We don't sell consumer health data, and we never will without first getting your signed, written authorization, as Washington's law requires. We have never sold it.

Your rights

  • To confirm and access: to know whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, to get a copy of it, and to get a list of every third party and affiliate we've shared it with, with their contact information. In the app: Settings, then Your privacy, then Download my data. It's free for everyone, and the file includes the list, the routes kept on your phone, the server's daily check of each day's result, your sick days, and your cheers and gifts. For your usage events and crash reports, email us and we'll send them.
  • To delete it: Settings, then Delete my account, or write to us. We delete it from our server (and so from our server host, Supabase) right away, and from the server's backups within 30 days. What friends saw while you were sharing stops showing once it's deleted.
  • To withdraw your consent to collecting or sharing it, anytime, in Settings, then Your privacy.
  • To not be treated differently for using any of these rights.

How to make a request, and appeal

Email privacy@playtend.app, or use Contact us in the app. Write from your account's email, or tell us your username, so we can check it's you. We'll answer within 45 days (and if we need up to 45 more, we'll tell you why). Requests are free.

If we turn down your request, reply to our answer and ask us to look again. We'll answer your appeal within 45 days and explain our decision. If we deny it, you can contact your state attorney general. In Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint. In Nevada: ag.nv.gov. In Connecticut: portal.ct.gov/ag.

How we protect it

It travels encrypted, only your account can read your backup, and only the people named above ever get any of it. If there's ever a breach, we'll tell you and the Federal Trade Commission within 60 days, sooner where a state requires it (see our privacy policy).

Changes

If we want to collect consumer health data for a new purpose, or share it in a new way, we'll ask for your consent first. The date at the top says when this page last changed.
